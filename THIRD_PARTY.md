# Third-party code

`firmware/vendor/scd4x/` contains the Sensirion SCD4x I²C driver at commit
`b52cebe1bb1b7050feaac75d7cd33e56c6a8a4e9` from
[Sensirion/embedded-i2c-scd4x](https://github.com/Sensirion/embedded-i2c-scd4x).
Its BSD-3-Clause license and copyright notices are preserved in that directory.

ESP-IDF and the locked Espressif MQTT/cJSON components are downloaded by the
build system and retain their respective licenses; they are not bundled here.
